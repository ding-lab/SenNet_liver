# This repository accompanies the study by Karpova, Li, Peng, and colleagues in Cell Genomics (2026). The paper maps cellular senescence across human liver aging, fibrosis, and colorectal cancer liver metastasis using single-cell and spatial measurements.

Publication: Karpova A, Li X, Peng C-W, et al. Cellular senescence in human liver under normal aging and cancer. Cell Genomics. 2026;6(2):101133. DOI: 10.1016/j.xgen.2025.101133 · PubMed: 41576948 · Open-access article
